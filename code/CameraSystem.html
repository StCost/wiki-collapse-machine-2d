    NameИмяValueЗначениеNoteЗаметка
    FollowRate3.5fExponential approach rate (1/s): higher = tighter. At steady speed the camera trails by speed / rate: ~1.3 m behind a walking pawn, ~4 m behind a truck at 14 m/s.
    ClampFraction0.8fFraction of the half view the target may lag behind before the camera is clamped to it.
    ScopeDeadZone0.25fThe cursor has to be past this share of the way from the screen centre to an edge before Ctrl starts to shift the view.
    FreeLookPercent25fCtrl looks this far (percent of the way to the screen edge) even with no scope. A scope that reaches further still wins.
