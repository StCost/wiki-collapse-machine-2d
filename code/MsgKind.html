    MsgKind

    src/DustConvoy.Sim/Net/Protocol.cs

    Wire protocol shared by every transport (LAN sockets, Steam P2P, in-memory loopback). One byte kind, then kind-specific payload. Poses are 30 Hz. The quiet world is sent when it changes, and at least once a second. Shots and sounds are their own packet. Enemy deltas are 10 Hz. Join chunks and hellos go reliable.
