    Pawn statuses, which items or events apply them, and what they do. Generated from the game data.Состояния персонажа, чем они вызываются и что делают. Собрано из данных игры.

    Statuses come from consumable doses, food, implants, weather, and a few scripted buffs. Detoxin clears alcohol / Drunk and the crash (hangover) that follows a combat stim; it does not remove antitoxin immunity, heat resistance, or a stim that is still running.

    Stealth, notice, and cover

    • Skill Stealth (quieter steps and swings) slows how fast a hostile fills its notice meter, and shortens footstep noise with the Quiet / Loud traits.
    • Skill View on the observer speeds that same meter. Animals see a shorter distance and notice more slowly.
    • Crouching (stealth stance) shrinks the observer's cone and range and lengthens notice time; in darkness a crouch can skip the check.
    • Items: Stealth field (gear_stealth) and Camouflage projector (mod_camo) can hide a still body. A stealth brain burner raises the Stealth skill.
    • Light level (night, unlit rooms, unpowered lamps), distance, and standing in a bush also slow notice. A stealth field is broken for a few seconds after you act loudly.
